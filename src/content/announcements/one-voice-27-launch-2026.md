The leadership of the **Kenya Coast Field (KCF)** is pleased to announce the official launch of **One Voice 27: Mission For All**, taking place on **Sabbath, 5th September 2026** across all churches, companies, and districts.

Under the overarching banner of **#AllThingsNew**, this initiative mobilizes every believer, youth, leader, and family across our territory to unite with one voice in proclaiming the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ.

---

### **Key Highlights & Purpose**

- **One Jesus:** Centering our life, faith, and message on Jesus Christ our Lord and Savior.
- **One Message:** Proclaiming the Three Angels' Messages with love, urgency, and coastal cultural relevance.
- **One Global Movement:** Connecting coastal Kenya with the worldwide mission of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

---

### **Participating Districts & Regions**

The launch convocation unites all pastorates and regions across the 10 counties of our Field:
- **Mombasa & Mombasa West**
- **South Coast (Kwale)**
- **Malindi, Kilifi & Galana**
- **Lamu & Mainland**
- **Taita Taveta**
- **Mtito Andei & Frontier Borders**
- **Northeastern Frontier (Garissa, Wajir, Mandera)**

---

### **How to Participate**

1. **Local Church Services:** Join your local Sabbath congregation on 5th September 2026 for dedicated launch services and mission commitment.
2. **Media & Broadcast:** Tune in to **Sheki FM Coastal Radio** for special launch programming and live field coverage.
3. **Prayer & Action:** Engage in local community outreach, prayer bands, and youth mobilization tracks under your District Pastor's coordination.
