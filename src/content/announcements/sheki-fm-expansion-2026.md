The Communication Department of the Kenya Coast Field is pleased to announce the successful licensing and expansion of Sheki FM radio frequencies to Voi (Taita Taveta) and Mokowe (Lamu). 

This expansion is part of the strategic mission plan to reach every coastal community with the message of hope.

### New Frequencies:
- **Taita Taveta**: 106.8 FM
- **Lamu Island & Mainland**: 107.2 FM
